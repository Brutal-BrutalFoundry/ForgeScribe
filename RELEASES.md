# ForgeScribe version history

## 1.0.1.0

Microsoft Store availability was verified on November 6, 2026. This is the public Windows version documented in the product facts.

- Local audio and video transcription with editable timed text.
- Microphone and system-audio recording, with provisional live caption previews.
- Transcript and subtitle exports, including SRT and VTT.
- Recovery checkpoints, optional speaker labels, and compatible GGML model import.
- Twelve menu languages.

Requires Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11 on x64 hardware. Speech-model downloads and Microsoft Store licensing may require internet. Caption previews may lag or revise words; generated transcripts need review. Accuracy and speed depend on the recording, model, and hardware.
