ForgeScribe by Brutal Foundry LLC
Product facts, November 6, 2026

Turn recordings into text. Keep them on your PC.

ForgeScribe turns interviews, lectures, voice notes and videos into editable text and subtitles with local speech recognition on Windows.

Website and real-result walkthrough: https://brutalfoundry.com/forgescribe
Microsoft Store: https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NJG5MVSJ582
Privacy: https://brutalfoundry.com/forgescribe-privacy
Public product information: https://github.com/Brutal-BrutalFoundry/ForgeScribe
Product support: support@brutalfoundry.com
Business contact: brutal@brutalfoundry.com

Price: US$14.99 one-time purchase. Seven-day Microsoft Store trial. Local pricing and trial terms are displayed by Microsoft. No subscription or separate ForgeScribe account.

Platform: Windows 10 version 22H2 or Windows 11, x64. Version: 1.0.1.0.

Workflow: Add audio/video or record a microphone/system-audio output, choose a local speech model, transcribe, review and edit, then export.

Exports: TXT, SRT, VTT, DOCX, Markdown, CSV, JSON, LRC and PDF.

Models: Fast, Balanced, Accurate or Best; compatible whisper.cpp GGML .bin model import. Models require an initial download. Microsoft Store licensing may require internet.

Limits: Live captions are provisional and can lag or revise words. Recognition, translation and optional speaker labels can be wrong. Review important results. PDF has limited character coverage. An active Store license is required to open the app and its saved transcript history; original recordings and exported files remain usable outside the app.
